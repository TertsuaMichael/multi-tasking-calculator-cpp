# Multi Tasking Calculator (C++)

A menu-driven console app I built while learning C++. One program, nine everyday tools, made with Nigerian use cases in mind.

## What it does

1. **Household & Utility:** LPG gas price (with VAT) and DisCo prepaid meter cost
2. **Freelance & Salary:** hours to pay, pay vs. minimum wage, overtime
3. **Health & Fitness:** steps to calories burned, and calories eaten to steps needed
4. **Academic Grading:** marks to grade and grade point
5. **Temperature Converter:** Fahrenheit and Celsius
6. **Naira-FX:** Naira to 11 currencies
7. **BMI Calculator:** BMI with a basic remark
8. **Tax Calculator (Demo):** a simple deductions demo
9. **Millions Yanga Shuffle Draw:** a guess-the-number raffle game
10. **Exit**

## Built with

C++ in Dev-C++.
